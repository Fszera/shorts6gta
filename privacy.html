Summary

Shorts6GTA Postagem runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It does not have users other than the owner, does not collect data from viewers, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): the owner's basic profile (open ID and display name) and permission to upload videos to the owner's own account (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram / Meta: permission to publish Reels and read insights of the channel's own professional account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload and schedule videos on the channel's own account.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only in local files on the owner's computer and are never sent to any server other than the official APIs of TikTok, Meta and Google. Video files are deleted from the computer shortly after they are published.

What we do not do

  • No tracking, analytics cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • No access to other people's accounts, messages or followers' data.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps; Meta: Business Settings → System users; Google: Account → Security → Third-party access).
